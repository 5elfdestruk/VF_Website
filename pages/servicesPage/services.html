01 — Personal

Cover, Savings & Wealth for You.

From a first funeral policy to a fully structured investment portfolio, these are the products that protect your family, grow your wealth and carry you through every stage of life.

Personal Families, graduates, professionals & retirees
01

Life, Risk & Funeral Cover

Funeral Products

A suite of funeral cover options structured for different household sizes, income levels and family compositions. Policies cover the principal member, spouse, children and extended family under a single premium, with benefits paid within 24–48 hours of a valid claim.

Limited Underwritten Life Cover

Life protection with a simplified underwriting process, making cover accessible to clients who may not qualify for fully underwritten policies. Pays a lump-sum death benefit to nominated beneficiaries.

Unlimited Life Cover

Fully underwritten life cover with no benefit ceiling. Benefit amounts calculated against real obligations — outstanding debt, income replacement, education funding and estate costs.

Disability & Income Protection

Covers permanent loss of physical function and income incapacity. Available as a lump sum for permanent disability or as monthly income protection replacing up to 75% of income.

Severe Illness Cover

Lump sum paid on diagnosis of a qualifying critical illness. The benefit is used as the client chooses — bridging treatment gaps, funding time away from work, or servicing obligations during recovery.

02

Savings & Retirement

Affordable Savings Products

Savings vehicles for building a financial buffer or working toward a specific goal. Regular and flexible premium options keep contributions sustainable.

Retirement Annuities

Contributions are deductible from taxable income. Growth is free of dividends and capital gains tax, with the fund used to purchase an income at retirement.

Living Annuities

Post-retirement income drawn from invested capital, with the balance continuing to grow in a chosen portfolio. Flexible drawdown within prescribed limits; residual capital remains in the estate.

Retirement Planning

Full retirement structuring — accumulation, preservation and income draw-down — built around current position, projected income needs and tax exposure across the appropriate vehicles.

03

Investments

Tax-Free Savings Accounts

All growth, income and withdrawals are free of tax. Most effective as a long-term wealth building tool used alongside other structured vehicles.

Endowment Policies

A five-year minimum investment taxed at the flat fund rate. After the term, withdrawals are tax-free and proceeds can be directed to beneficiaries outside the estate without executor fees.

Wealth & Unit Trust Investments

Access to multi-manager platforms across growth, balanced and income portfolios. Strategy aligned to risk profile, time horizon and financial objectives.

Employee Benefits

Pension and provident funds alongside group life and disability cover — a single group structure delivering meaningful protection at group rates, with simplified underwriting.

02 — Business & Medical

Protecting the Enterprise.

Commercial risk and healthcare structuring for businesses and their people — from keyman continuity to employee medical cover and asset protection.

Business Founders, executives & employee groups
04

Business Assurance

Keyman Cover

A policy owned by the business that pays a lump sum if a founder, key executive or revenue-generating specialist passes or becomes permanently disabled. Gives the business liquidity to continue operating.

Business Contingency Cover

Covers fixed overheads — salaries, rent, loan repayments — for a defined period after the loss of a key individual. Prevents forced liquidation of assets.

Buy and Sell Assurance

Funds a structured ownership transfer when a partner passes or can no longer continue. Surviving partners retain control; the deceased's estate receives fair value.

05

Medical Insurance

Primary Care Cover

Covers day-to-day medical expenses — GP consultations, chronic medication, basic dentistry and pathology. Access to routine healthcare without the full cost of a medical aid.

Gap Cover

Bridges the shortfall between what a medical aid pays at scheme rates and what a specialist charges. Absorbs the balance so the client carries no unexpected liability.

Group Medical Insurance

Employer-sponsored health cover delivering primary care and gap benefits to employee groups. A workforce benefit enhancement where a full medical scheme isn't cost-effective.
