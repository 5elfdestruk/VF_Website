These are illustrative projected capital-depletion periods under fixed 7% and 9% nominal annual investment-growth scenarios. Income is paid monthly, investment growth is applied first, and monthly income increases annually by 5% inflation.

These calculations are estimates for educational and illustrative purposes only. They are based on assumptions about investment returns, inflation and contributions, which may change over time. Actual investment performance is not guaranteed, and fees, taxes and individual circumstances may affect the outcome. This calculator does not constitute financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Please consult a suitably qualified financial adviser before making financial decisions.
