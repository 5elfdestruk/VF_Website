The Opportunity

Varied Remuneration.

Two flexible earning structures designed to reward production and grow with you.

Option 01

Fixed + Bonus

A fixed monthly component plus a performance-based bonus, tied to your production targets.

  • Predictable monthly income
  • Performance bonus on targets
  • Ideal for advisors building a book
Option 02

Commission Based

Commission split that grows in your favour as you close more business.

  • Split from 50/50 to 80/20
  • Uncapped earning potential
  • Split improves with production
